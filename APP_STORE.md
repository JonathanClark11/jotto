## Description
Cinq is a word guessing game with a twist. Instead of color-coded tiles, you get back a number — how many letters in your guess appear in the secret five-letter word. Use logic, elimination, and deduction to crack it.

**Three ways to play:**
- **Daily** — one word for everyone, every day. Solve it and see how your result stacks up against other players.
- **Solo** — practice anytime with a random word.
- **Rival** — challenge a friend head-to-head. You pick a word for them; they pick one for you. First to crack it wins.

**Smart deduction tools:**
- Mark letters you've confirmed are in the word
- Cross out letters you've eliminated
- Group letters together when a guess narrows down exactly how many of them appear

Cinq is free, has no ads, requires no account, and works great offline for Solo and Daily play.
